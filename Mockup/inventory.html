The Wednesday Itihaas Class — a documented curriculum

At Dashmesh Gurdwara, children present creative homework projects each week. Groups have names — the Nagni Group and the Khanda Group. Recorded projects include:

  • A working clay well, built for a sakhi
  • Chamkaur di Garhi recreated in LEGO, with clay hills and LED lights for the eternal jot
  • A 3D model of the Janam Asthan at Nankana Sahib
  • The Kodhe Rakhash sakhi as a 3D model
  • Guru Nanak Dev Ji's return to Talwandi; Raja Shivnabh's three tests
  • Kasht Yog and Bhagat Yog explained by the children themselves
  • A hand fan, a map model, and a ladoo-making seva class
This is the single most website-ready thing I have found. It is a real curriculum, taught weekly, documented with photographs, and it shows children doing something recognisable and joyful. It belongs on the Itihaas class page almost verbatim.

Also confirmed

  • Baal Updesh collection published — three books, March 2026
  • Kirtan class repertoire — Raag Basant in Kaumb Taal · Raag Jaijaiwanti Guldasta and Partaal
  • MMA is taught alongside Santhiya, Kirtan, Tabla and Jori
