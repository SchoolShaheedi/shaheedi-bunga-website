Santhiyaਸੰਥਿਆ

Learning to read Gurbani correctly — every letter, every vishraam, every sound placed where it belongs.

You do not need to read Gurmukhi to start. If you have never written a letter of it in your life, you begin at the muharni, below, and you are taught the letters from nothing.

Adults are welcome. Most people who write to us are in their twenties and thirties and assume this is a children's class. It is not.

Where everyone starts

The muharni.

Thirty-five letters, each sounded through every vowel sign in turn, until the sounds are automatic and no longer need thinking about. This table is the first thing a student is given and the last thing they stop returning to.

Choose any row to hear it in your head at full size.

ਮੁਹਾਰਨੀ · Muharni

35 akhar × 10 laga matra

Select a row above.

The hatched cells are not omissions. ੳ, ਅ and ੲ are vowel carriers, not consonants — they take only three or four signs each. Learning which is part of learning the table.

The muharni is set out in Baal Updesh, the primer Shaheedi Bunga prints. Students are given a copy; nothing here needs buying first.

What you learn, in order

From the letters
to laarivaar.

Santhiya is the slowest thing taught here, and it goes in a fixed order — you do not skip. One group finished their laarivaar santhiya after five years, which is the ordinary pace of it and the reason the class runs every morning rather than once a week.

The stages below are a placeholder. They are drawn from general Damdami Taksal sources, not from your ustads. Send me the real route and I will replace them.

YOU START HERE Muharniਮੁਹਾਰਨੀ

The 35 letters sounded through every vowel. No prior Gurmukhi assumed.

THEN Akhar & laga matraਅੱਖਰ · ਲਗਾਂ ਮਾਤਰਾਂ

Writing the letters, and the vowel signs as marks rather than sounds.

THEN Pothiਪੋਥੀ

First continuous reading, out loud, one line at a time with an ustad.

THEN Nitnemਨਿਤਨੇਮ

The daily banis, corrected word by word until the ucharan holds.

THEN Larger banisਸੁਖਮਨੀ ਸਾਹਿਬ

Longer paath, where vishraam and breath start to matter as much as sound.

EVENTUALLY Laarivaarਲੜੀਵਾਰ

Reading Gurbani as it was written, unbroken, without the word spacing.
