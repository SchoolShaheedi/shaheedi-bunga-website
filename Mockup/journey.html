      ਪੋਥੀਆਂ ਤੇ ਗ੍ਰੰਥLibrary

      The granths, pothis and recordings we teach from — published free, for anyone, with no login and no charge.

      A bunga was never only a classroom; it held the books. This is ours. Everything here is either a granth our students read in class, a volume our vidhvaans reference in katha, or a recording of a talk we have already given.

      If a text is good enough to teach from, it is good enough to give away.

      ਸਹਿਯੋਗKeep every class free

      We’re volunteer-run. Donations pay for instruments, pothis, hall hire and camp places — never salaries.

      We have never charged for a class and we do not intend to start. That only works because the sangat pays for it instead — a jori costs what a jori costs whether or not the child holding it can afford one.

      Gift Aid adds 25p to every pound at no cost to you.

      ਸਾਡੇ ਬਾਰੇAbout the school

      Shaheedi Bunga Gurmat Vidyala is a volunteer-run Sikh school in Leicester. We teach Gurbani santhia, kirtan on tanti saaj, jori, gatka, katha and Sikh history — across the city’s gurdwaras and online — and we have never charged anyone for any of it.

      Education is what nurtures and strengthens the next generation, mentally and physically.

      The aim is narrow and specific: that a child can read Gurbani correctly in larreevaar, sing it in its raag on a tanti saaj instrument, keep taal on the jori, stand in the akhaara, and know the history they came from. Everything we run serves one of those five things.

      How it works

      Weekly classes run through the year at the gurdwaras. Online classes run for people who cannot get to Leicester — which now includes students well outside the UK. Once a year the Gurbani Vidhya Camp condenses all of it into two weeks, with visiting vidhvaans teaching katha, kirtan and shastar vidhya.

      Everything we teach from is published in the library, and the katha we record goes out on YouTube — including a separate channel for children.

      Who teaches

      Volunteers. Some are ustaads in their own right, some are former students now teaching the class below them. Anyone working with under-18s is DBS-checked.

      Some of this wording is drafted rather than approved — the founding story, dates and named teachers should come from you.
