Sikhi Calendar · ਸਿੱਖੀ ਕੈਲੰਡਰ

Privacy
policy.

The Sikhi Calendar app does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Last updated 5 October 2026

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter — your own events, reminders and settings — is kept only on your phone. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. If you uninstall the app, this information is removed with it.

Sharing

When you choose to share a day (as a picture card or as text) or export a file, it goes only where you send it, through your phone’s own share menu. The app does not see or keep a copy.

Internet

The app works without an internet connection; all dates are worked out on the phone. It contacts a server only if one of the charity’s volunteers signs in under Settings → Admin to edit the calendar. That sends only the calendar edits they make (names and dates of purabs), with no personal information about anyone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
