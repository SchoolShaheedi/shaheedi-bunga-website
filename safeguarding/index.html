Raising a
concern.

Children and adults learn here. If something about a child’s safety or welfare worries you — at a class, a camp or a samagam — please say so. You do not need to be certain, and you do not need to have proof.

If someone is in immediate danger

Call 999. Do not wait to tell us first, and do not wait to be sure. The police and the council can act straight away; we cannot.

Telling us

Speak to whoever is teaching the class, or write to gurmat@shaheedibunga.com. Say what you saw or heard, when, and who was there. You can ask us to keep your name out of it, and we will unless the law or a child’s safety makes that impossible.

We will come back to you. If you would rather not raise it with us at all — for any reason, including if your concern is about one of our own volunteers — use one of the numbers below instead. They are not a last resort; they are a proper route and they are always open to you.

Going outside the charity

Emergency
999 A child is in immediate danger, or a crime is happening now.
Police, non-urgent
101 Something has happened but nobody is in danger right now.
Leicester City Council

NSPCC helpline
0808 800 5000 Free, and you can stay anonymous. For adults worried about a child.
Childline
0800 1111 For children and young people, free and confidential.

Photographs of children

This website and our social media show photographs of classes and camps, and children appear in some of them. If your child is in one and you would rather they were not, tell us and we will take it down. You do not have to give a reason, and it changes nothing about their place in the class.

Where we are with this

A written safeguarding policy is being prepared for the trustees to adopt, and this page will carry it when it is in place, along with the name of the person responsible. We would rather tell you that plainly than leave the page looking more complete than it is — and in the meantime every route above works today.
