Shaheedi Bunga · Leicester

A bunga was a house beside the water at Amritsar where Sikhs were taught to read Gurbani correctly, to hold a shastar, and to sing the raags as they were given. More than seventy of them stood there. Almost all were pulled down.

We carry the name of one of them. The building is gone; the teaching was never the building.

Why

The subject is a school of reading. Making the page itself a folio — Gurmukhi leading, red rubrication, marginalia, a folio number — says that without a single image. It is the cheapest to build and the fastest to load of anything here, and it cannot break.

The trade-off

Quiet to the point of austere, and it asks the visitor to read. A fourteen-year-old scrolling from Instagram may not stop for it.
