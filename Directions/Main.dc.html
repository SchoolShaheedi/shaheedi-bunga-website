The drawing inks itself in as you scroll · each chapter draws a different building

Why

A drawing is never wrong. Every problem with the 3D — proportions, ornament, the glow on the gold — disappears the moment the building is a line rather than a surface. It is also unmistakably yours, and it loads instantly on a phone.

The trade-off

It is quieter. No depth, no reflection, no moving through space — the drama has to come from the ink appearing and the typography, not from the camera.
