Why

The old site dead-ended every class button on an empty contact page. This is the fix: the single most useful fact — what is on tonight and where — is the first thing anyone sees. Built to convert 3,626 Instagram followers into students.

No romance. It says nothing about the martyrs, the bungas or Amritsar above the fold — and it only works if the timetable is kept accurate, forever.
