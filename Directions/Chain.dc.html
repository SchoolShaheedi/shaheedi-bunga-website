Nothing here was written down. It was handed over.

A bunga is not a place. It is a chain of people who each learned from someone and then taught someone else. This is ours, so far.

Join the chain See every teacher

Why

It turns the thing that makes you unusual — students who learned here now teaching in eight countries — from a list into the structure of the page. It is guru-shishya parampara drawn out, and no other Sikh charity site looks like it.

The trade-off

It only works if it is true and specific. It needs real names, real places and permission from every person on it — and it looks thin until there are enough of them.
