# The real timetable — from the charity's own poster

Found 2026-08-26 in the Instagram archive: a designed **"WEEKLY TIMETABLE — LEICESTER"**
poster (`Assets/Instagram/timetable-poster-gtb.jpg`, posted 12 Apr 2025 alongside the
"Weekly Sikhi Classes" caption). It supersedes everything reconstructed from captions.

**Venue:** Guru Tegh Bahadur Gurudwara, 106 E Park Road, Leicester LE5 4QB
**Tel:** +44 7394 848407 · **Email:** gurmat@shaheedibunga.com · **Web:** www.shaheedibunga.com
**Strapline on the poster:** "Connecting the next Generation to Sikhi"

| Day | Morning | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | 7–9am Santhiya | 5–8pm Santhiya · Kirtan/Tanti Saaj · Tabla · Violin — 6:30–7:30pm Monday Night Talks (pizza after) |
| **Tuesday** | 7–9am Santhiya | 5–6pm Santhiya · Nitnem Kanth Class |
| **Wednesday** | 7–9am Santhiya | 5–6pm Itihaas Class (Sri Nanak Parkash) · Kirtan Class |
| **Thursday** | 7–9am Santhiya | 5–6pm Santhiya · Kirtan Class |
| **Friday** | 7–9am Santhiya | 5–6pm Santhiya · Kirtan Class |
| **Saturday** | 9–11:30am Santhiya | 2–6pm Air Rifling · 4–6pm Santhiya · 6–8pm Gatka · Kirtan/Tanti Saaj |
| **Sunday** | 9–11:30am Santhiya | 12:30–3pm Advanced Gatka · Kirtan/Tanti Saaj · Katha Class · 2:30–4pm Archery · 4–6pm Santhiya |

## What this corrects

- **Santhiya runs every single day** — 7–9am on all five weekdays, 9–11:30am at weekends,
  plus an evening session most days. It is far more than the caption suggested.
- **Nitnem Kanth Class** (Tuesday 5–6pm) was not known at all.
- **Violin** is confirmed as a Monday class.
- **Air Rifling** has a standing Saturday 2–6pm slot — it is a weekly class, not just a
  Hola Mohalla discipline.
- **Advanced Gatka** is a separate Sunday session from the Saturday beginners class.
- **Archery** is Sunday 2:30–4pm here, not "6pm onwards" as older posts said.

## Still not on the poster

Age groups · fees (no post has ever mentioned one) · teacher names per class ·
whether it is still current in 2026 (the poster is from April 2025) ·
the Dashmesh Darbar and Holy Bones timetables — this poster is **Guru Tegh Bahadur only**.

## Two defects in the poster itself

1. **"GURDWARAR"** — typo for Gurdwara, on both Santhiya Camp posters too.
2. The Gurmukhi tuk on the camp posters renders **without word spaces** and with mangled
   matras: `ਗੁਰਪਰਸਾਦੀਵਿਦਿਆਵੀਚਾਰੈਪੜਿਪੜਿਪਾਵੈਮਾਨੁ`. The caption has it correctly as
   **ਗੁਰਪਰਸਾਦੀ ਵਿਦਿਆ ਵੀਚਾਰੈ ਪੜਿ ਪੜਿ ਪਾਵੈ ਮਾਨੁ ॥** — a font-shaping failure in Canva.
   Worth telling whoever makes the posters.
