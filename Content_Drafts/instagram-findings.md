## 6. Events and camps — the eight-year record

**Recurring, in order of how established they are:**

1. **Patshahi Dasvi National Hola Mohalla UK** — the flagship. 2023 (1 Apr, Gur Panth
   Parkash), 2025 (23 Mar, Gur Panth Parkash), 2026 (7 Mar, Dashmesh Darbar). Gatka,
   tag archery, horse riding, axe throwing, rifle shooting, knife and chakar throwing.
2. **Gurbani Vidhya Camp** — a two-week annual intensive at GTB. 2022 (Paath Bodh of
   Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji), 2023 (7–20 Aug, Vaaran Bhai Gurdas Ji), 2024 (5–18 Aug,
   Kabit/Svaiye). The most serious teaching the charity does.
3. **Gurbani Santhiya Camp** — Feb 2026 (16–19), Mar/Apr 2026 (30 Mar–3 Apr, 9 AM–12 PM),
   May 2026 (25–29, 3–7 PM). All at Dashmesh Darbar.
4. **Sikhi Camp (kids, 5+)** — 2023, Feb 2025 (22 Feb, Dashmesh, 9–4), Apr 2025
   (15–17 Apr, GTB, 9–4), Oct 2024 (25–26 Oct, Holy Bones, 9–4). **April 2025 drew 190 kids.**
5. **Monday Night Talks** — weekly through spring 2025 at GTB, 6:30–7:30 PM, pizza langar.
   Speakers from Basics of Sikhi, Sikh2Inspire, SYUK, Jyoti Saroop Foundation and in-house.
6. **25 Singh Lareevaar Sri Akhand Paath Samagam** — Dec 2021, Dec 2022 at Gur Panth
   Parkash; Aug 2021 at Guru Nanak Gurdwara Smethwick. Includes Amrit Sanchar.
7. **Gatka Fight Night** — first UK inter-akhara fight night, 4 May 2024, GTB, in memory
   of Ustad Bhai Charnjit Singh Ji's 20th barsi.
8. **National Sikh Archery Tournament** — 15 Feb 2025, GTB. Ages 10–15 (10 m), 16+ (15 m).

## 8. Audience — who the site is actually for

From `audience_insights.html`, 27 May – 24 Aug 2026:

- **3,631 followers**, +2.1% on the previous quarter.
- **UK 52.7%** · India 27.1% · Canada 7.9% · USA 4.4% · Australia 1.6%.
- **Leicester 7.5%** · Birmingham 3.8% · Wolverhampton 2.2% · Delhi 2.2% · Ludhiana 2.2%.
- **Age:** 25–34 is 39.7%, 35–44 is 29.1%, 18–24 is 15.5%. Only 1.2% are 13–17.
- **75.6% men, 24.3% women.**

**This changes a design assumption.** The audience is not teenagers — it is parents
in their late twenties to forties, half of them outside the UK, only about one in
thirteen actually in Leicester. The site's job for the overseas half is *not* the
Leicester timetable; it is the library, the recordings and the apps. That argues for
making the **online offering** in the bio (Santya, Tanti Saaj Kirtan, Jori, Katha) a
first-class part of phase 1, not a later phase.

Engagement over the same quarter: 17,113 content interactions, **8,142 accounts
engaged (+58.9%)**, of which **91.4% were non-followers** — reels reach far beyond the
follower list (10,228 reel interactions vs 5,959 post interactions). But only
**7 external link taps** in three months. The Instagram audience is not currently
clicking through to anything, because there has been nothing worth clicking to.

**Best-performing posts** (`Instagram/ig-performance.json`, 221 posts with insights,
