Vidyala

How it publishes

the model

One naming rule, applied everywhere

Shaheedi Bunga [House] [Discipline] — always in that order, always with the charity name attached.

Shaheedi Bunga Kirtan Vidyala. Shaheedi Bunga Gatka Akhara. Shaheedi Bunga Archery Academy. The archive has been doing this inconsistently for eight years — Vidayala, Vidiyala, Vidhyala, Vidyalai all appear — and fixing the spelling is a one-line decision that makes everything else searchable.

The single exception is Patshahi Dasvi National Hola Mohalla UK, which already stands alone and should keep doing so.

URL architecture

House, then discipline. It mirrors the structure above, keeps every slug short, and means a new discipline never needs a new top-level decision:

  • /vidyala/santhiya, /vidyala/kirtan, /vidyala/jalandhar
  • /akhara/gatka, /akhara/archery, /akhara/shastar-vidya
  • /camps/, /samagam/, /events/, /talks/, /prakashan/, /tools/
  • /timetable — one page, filterable by house, venue and age. Not a page per venue.

Fix the nav first. The current draft has both "Classes" and "Akharas & Vidyalas", which are the same thing. Six items, no overlap:

Vidyala · Akhara · Library · Events · About · Donate

One visual system, one 3D scene

Do not build a WebGL scene per division. The parikrama scene is already around 700 meshes with a full mirrored copy and six post-processing passes, and its performance on real phones is still unverified. Nine of those is nine things to build and nine to break.

Instead: every division is a different bunga on the same ring. Give each house a fixed camera position in the one scene that already exists and render a still frame for its page header.

The Kirtan Vidyala is the bunga with lamplight in the arches. The Gatka Akhara is the open ground at the water's edge. The Jalandhar Vidyala is the bunga seen from across the sarovar. One model, one performance budget — and the pages are coherent because they are literally in the same place. That argument for the structure is stronger than any set of logos would be.

Live WebGL stays on the homepage only. The thirteen models already built — harmandir, akal takht, three bunga variants, ramgarhia bunga, darshani deori, causeway, sarovar, parikrama, nishan sahib, lamp standard — are enough to frame every one of them.

Sub-brand marks: don't

Nine houses do not need nine logos. One wordmark, one engraved gold band from the existing roundel, and a house name set in the same face. A discipline earns a mark when it outgrows the charity — which, on this evidence, has happened exactly once, to Hola Mohalla.

Instagram stays one account

91.4% of engaged accounts are non-followers, so reach comes from reels travelling, not from the follower list. A second account starts from zero and halves the cadence on the first. @shaheedi_bunga_kids already proves it — it exists, and it is mentioned three times in eight years.

The story highlights are already the taxonomy. Keep one account and let the website do the separating.

What earns its own page

Score each candidate out of five. Four or more, it gets a page; under that, it is a section on its parent.

  1. Its own ustad, not shared with another discipline
  2. A regular slot that has run for twelve months or more
  3. An audience who would not come to the other things
  4. A named person to run its communications
  5. Something new to say at least monthly

Gatka and Archery clear it comfortably. The Archery League scored two, and that is why it is in the dormant register.

Launch sequence

  1. Timetable and contactEvery house listed, one filterable timetable, gurmat@shaheedibunga.com on a contact page that finally has contact details. This alone fixes the old site's worst failure — every class button dead-ended on an empty page.

Governance flags

answer before publishing

Four things on this page cannot go live as they stand. None of them is a design problem — they are decisions for the trustees.

1 · The Jalandhar vidyala

A July 2021 post describes it plainly: residential for under-16s, with school fees, clothes and food provided by sangat. That is a UK charity funding a residential children's facility overseas.

It carries Charity Commission duties on overseas expenditure, partner due diligence and safeguarding, and it cannot be described as "former students teaching independently". Check it against the registered objects and the trustees' position before a single word about it is published.

2 · Photographs of children

The archive is 5,429 media files, and a large share is children's classes and camps. There is no photo-consent or safeguarding policy on record. Nothing from the archive goes on the site until there is a written position — and phase 1 includes a safeguarding page anyway.

3 · The worldwide teaching claim

Eight years of posts evidence a Netherlands camp and a Scotland tour. Kenya, Germany, Kuwait and Australia appear nowhere. Those came from conversation, not from the record — they need sourcing, and the wording must stay "students teaching independently", never anything implying the charity operates overseas.

4 · Two loose ends

Sikhi Calendar App — the March 2025 update post reads as ownership, the Linktree treats it as third-party. Resolve which.

The source library — whether the scanned granths can be published publicly is still open, and it gates the entire Library section.
