Every class is free. Instruments are lent out, granths are shared, and nobody is paid — not a teacher, not a trustee. What the charity spends goes on the things the classes need.

Where the money goes

Shaheedi Bunga is a Charitable Incorporated Organisation, registered since 2019 and up to date with its reporting. It owns no building and leases none; the gurdware give the space. No trustee receives any payment or benefit, and there are no salaries of any size on the register.

Seva in kind

Instruments are lent to students who do not have their own, so a dilruba, taus or tabla in working order goes straight into a child’s hands. The same is true of granths and pothis. Ask before buying anything — what is short changes term to term.
